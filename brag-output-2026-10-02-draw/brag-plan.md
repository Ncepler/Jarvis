# Reel: "Draw your business" (stickman-inspired idea #26, roll-call · COMMENT)

One of the 10 stickman-inspired ideas (position 26, local copy, no Supabase this batch). Built 2026-10-02, second batch, /brag (brag-slim path) + canvas. **Not** a Gemini Omni clip. 16s.

## Where the stickman skill came in (ideas only)
Hand-drawn line devices with line boil, a visual change every 2–3s, a flat white canvas, one ink + one accent (rose), and a closing beat that asks the viewer in.

## Angle
At 120 BPM, each trade gets 5 beats. A pen draws an icon (scissors, a flower, a mower, a cake, a hammer) with its trade name in handwriting. The icon
opens with a circular iris into that trade's **real demo hero** (barber, florist, lawn care, bakery, renovation; Playwright captures) and closes back.
Then a blank canvas, the pen hovers: "yours? / comment what you'd draw ↓".

Honesty: every site shown is a demo style, and the demo headers say DEMO BUILD. A line on screen says so too.
Sound: a marker scratch while drawing, one rising pluck chord per reveal, an iris whoosh in and out, a light kick on the beat. -15 LUFS. Poster = frame 120 (the florist reveal).

## Rebuild
From `work/`: `python3 -m http.server 8921`, `PORT=8921 node render.mjs events`, `python3 sound.py`, `PORT=8921 node render.mjs video video.mp4`, `bash finish.sh 120`.
