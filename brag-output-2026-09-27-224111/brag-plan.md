# /brag plan: "Pick a card" (Vilas Studio reel #2)

## Answers
- **What is it?** Vilas Studio builds websites for local service businesses.
- **Who's it for / what does it do for them?** Owners whose customers Google them before they call. The site decides who gets that call.
- **What sets it apart?** Every niche gets its own style (florist, barber, bakery, landscaper, auto body…), fit by hand.
- **Most impressive / funniest claim:** Vilas built a real magician's site whose copy says "Pick a card." So the studio performs the trick itself.
- **Visual hook:** five playing cards dealt face-up into a spotlight. Their faces are the real niche photos from the demo sites. "Pick a card."
- **Real UI shown:** five live demo sites (florist, barber, bakery, landscaping, auto body) playing their real hero entrance inside phones, the VS brand mark on every card back, and the magician demo as a card face.
- **Tone:** freeform. A close-up magic act that turns into a promo. Closest preset is `cinematic`.
- **Share line:** "Pick a card. Remember it."

## Angle
The reel is a real card trick (the "all cards change" effect). The viewer picks a card, it vanishes, and it comes back as a website. Then the pitch, in the site's own words: "People Google you before they hire you. What they find decides who gets the call." Promotional rather than bragging, and built for rewatches and "wait, how?" comments. On a second look, every card has changed.

## Checked against `instagram_posts` (reel #1), nothing reused
Different hook (a participation trick, not "guess the price"). No price. No tagline payoff. No coverflow, odometer, tile outro, film grain, caption sticker, or bone/ink flip. New palette (warm spotlight stage) and a new score (120 BPM, D minor to F major house, not 90 BPM R&B).

## Format
Vertical 1080×1920, 30fps, 22.0s. 120 BPM: 1 beat = 15 frames, 1 bar = 60 frames.

## Honesty
- The card photos are the demos' real hero photos. The magician card is a capture of the real magician demo.
- The phones show real demo sites, with their "DEMO BUILD" pills visible.
- The pitch lines are the site's own copy (`COPY.hero.outcome`). No prices, stats or clients.

## Music
Original score, synthesized in numpy. Music box (inharmonic FM partials), Karplus-Strong pizzicato and harp, saw pads, FM electric-piano stabs, sub, and house drums with the kick sidechaining the pads. Progression: Dm(add9), B♭maj7, A7, Dm, A7sus4, then the drop: Fmaj9, Am7, B♭maj9, C6/9, and a final Fmaj9. Every card sound is placed from the composition's exported cue sheet. Mixed to -13.7 LUFS.
