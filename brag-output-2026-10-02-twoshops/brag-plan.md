# Reel: "Two shop owners" (stickman-inspired idea #27, comparison · SHARE)

One of the 10 stickman-inspired ideas (position 27, local copy, no Supabase this batch). Built 2026-10-02, second batch, /brag (brag-slim path) + canvas. **Not** a Gemini Omni clip. 16s.

## Where the stickman skill came in (ideas only)
Mirrored stacked choreography (two identical figures hammering in unison), a character walking between frames, two accents, and a fable-like single point.

## Angle
Two identical contractors, A (top) and B (bottom), same skill, hammering in sync, waving in sync. A customer holds up two phones. The top one is a
**drawn, generic line-art page** ("WELCOME!!!", "image not found", © 2015) and gets a red "?". The bottom one is the **real renovation demo**
(Maple & Main, a demo brand, a real scroll capture) and gets a green check. The customer walks down to B and shakes hands. A keeps hammering alone.
"same skill. / different first impression."

Honesty: the "bad" side is drawn line art, never a capture of a real business's site. A line on screen says so.
Sound: two music-box voices in unison that split (top slips minor, bottom resolves up), hammer taps (two, then one), a phone pop, steps, a handshake clap. -15 LUFS. Poster = frame 230 (both phones up).

## Rebuild
From `work/`: `python3 -m http.server 8922`, `PORT=8922 node render.mjs events`, `python3 sound.py`, `PORT=8922 node render.mjs video video.mp4`, `bash finish.sh 230`.
