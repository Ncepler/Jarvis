# Omni Flash production package — The Customer You Never Met

Phase B of the `directing-stickman-videos` skill: six standalone prompts for Gemini Omni Flash (on Higgsfield: `gemini_omni_flash_1_1`, `mode: text-to-video`, `duration: 10`, `aspect_ratio: 9:16`). Not generated yet: the account had 3.86 credits on 2026-10-01, and six 10s clips cost roughly 60 credits at 360p or about 180 at 720p. The reel in this folder (`brag.mp4`) is the same storyboard rendered locally with code instead.

## Global continuity block

- 9:16 vertical, light theme: black line art on a flat pure-white canvas.
- Characters: identical faceless stick figures (baker, customer, designer, crowd). Uniform medium line weight.
- Accents: warm gold (craft, value, the new site), electric blue (the phone search), vivid red (doubt, the lost sale).
- Narrator: bright, energetic adult female, natural American English. 133 words across six clips.
- Audio arc: sparse piano → near-silence on the reframe → warm synth build → resolving chord.
- Continuity: every clip ends on the exact frame the next one opens on (see stitching guide).

First frame: A completely blank pure-white frame; the scene begins immediately with the first beat.
[0–3s] Extreme close-up on a warm gold bread loaf resting on a shop counter, two thin black steam curls rising, soft warm gold rings pulsing outward around it. The camera pulls back smoothly to reveal a proud stick-figure baker behind the counter, one arm presenting the loaf, framed inside a line-drawn shop window under a scalloped awning.
[3–7s] The camera keeps pulling back and tilts down to the sidewalk, revealing the whole storefront: window, door with a small bell above it, awning, roofline. A stick-figure woman walks in from the right and stops just outside the door, facing the window. A faint vivid red outline copy of her lifts off her body and drifts upward, fading out.
[7–10s] She raises a phone to face height and its screen lights electric blue with soft blue rings. The camera pushes fast into the phone as a flat electric-blue circle grows from the screen until blue fills the entire frame.
Narrator (audio only): a bright, energetic adult female voice speaking natural American English, same voice in every clip, delivery quiet and confiding, with a small sting on "never know". Final frame: the frame is entirely filled with flat electric blue.
Strictly avoid: photorealism or 3D rendering; faces, hair or clothing; extra limbs, malformed or disconnected lines, changing proportions or line weight; any color beyond black, white and the three accents; extra characters beyond those described; any visible words, letters, numbers, captions, subtitles, interface text, logos, watermarks or color labels (all cards, screens and signs are icon-only); any added, omitted, repeated, reordered or paraphrased dialogue.

## Stitching guide

1 → 2: full electric-blue frame → same blue frame contracts into the phone. Hard cut on the blue.
2 → 3: highlighted card fills the frame → same card stretches into the old page. Match cut.
3 → 4: push into the window on the slumped baker → same baker, storefront fades. Match cut.
4 → 5: cracked red storefront fills the frame → same storefront, designer drops in. Match cut.
5 → 6: page shrinks into a gold phone in her hand → same gold phone, camera pulls back. Match cut.
Trim up to 0.3s at each head if a clip starts slowly, and add 4–6 frame audio crossfades at every cut.
