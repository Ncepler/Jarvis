## Never claim
Clients we don't have, testimonials, review counts, star ratings, "trusted by", "#1", "best in", guaranteed rankings, traffic, leads or revenue, "AI-built in minutes", years in business, team size. Don't show any real business's site as "bad", don't show any real person's likeness, and don't imply the original video's creator endorses Vilas.
