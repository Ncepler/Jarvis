# Reel: "Five stairs" (batch 3 · line art · SHARE)

Batch 3, built 2026-10-02 with /brag (brag-slim path) + canvas. No Supabase (not used this batch). Idea list: `reels-batch-3-ideas.md`.

## Angle
A staircase to a shop door where every step is a reason to leave (slow, no hours, no phone, looks old, unclear). The first stick figure trips on step three and gives up. The stairs redraw as a smooth ramp with hours, phone and photos ticked, and the next figure walks straight in as the door opens.
Honesty: entirely drawn, no real shop or stats; 'A drawn example' in the footer.
Sound: pen squeaks as the stairs draw, footsteps, a thud on the fall, tick bells on the ramp, door creak, end chord. -15 LUFS.
