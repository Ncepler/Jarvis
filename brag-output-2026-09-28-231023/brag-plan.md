# /brag plan: "The receipt" (Vilas Studio reel #3)

## Answers
- **What is it?** Vilas Studio builds websites for local service businesses.
- **Who's it for / what does it do for them?** Owners who don't have time for a web project. They send three things and get a finished site.
- **What sets it apart?** How little the owner has to do. The FAQ says it plainly: "Send your logo, photos, and hours. We write everything else."
- **Most impressive claim:** "Live in days, not months." (the How-it-works heading).
- **Visual hook:** a thermal receipt printing out line by line: WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU, then A LOGO ✓, A FEW PHOTOS ✓, YOUR HOURS ✓.
- **Real UI shown:** the bakery demo ("Golden Hour Bakehouse"), assembled piece by piece and playing its real hero entrance, and the real /start intake page.
- **Tone:** freeform. Warm, bright and tactile, a "look how easy" promo. Closest preset is `default`.
- **Share line:** "Everything we need to build your website fits on a receipt."

## Angle
Promote the process, not the price. It's the least you'll ever have to do for a website: three things on a receipt, and we build the rest.

## Checked against `instagram_posts` (reels #1–2), nothing reused
First light/bone-palette reel. It uses the untried "/start intake flow" angle. New devices: a printing receipt, a tear-off, objects flying into a site that assembles from them, a site built in reveal order, and an address-bar ending. No price, magic trick, coverflow, odometer, tile outro, card→phone morph or phone fan. The score is a new style too: 100 BPM, G major, kalimba and plucked guitar.

## Format
Vertical 1080×1920, 30fps, 21.6s. 100 BPM: 1 beat = 18 frames, 1 bar = 72 frames, 9 bars.

## Honesty
- Every line is real site copy: the FAQ ("Send your logo, photos, and hours. We write everything else."), the How-it-works heading ("Live in days, not months."), and the /start intro ("Takes about five minutes").
- The site is the real bakery demo with its "DEMO BUILD" pill visible.
- The "GH" badge and the hours card are illustrative props standing in for what an owner would send.
- The site's contact section says the form takes "two minutes" while /start says "about five minutes". The reel uses the /start wording.

## Music
Original numpy-synth score, 100 BPM, G major: Gmaj7, Em7, Cmaj9, D6, repeating, ending on Gmaj9. Kalimba (sine plus inharmonic partial), Karplus-Strong plucked bass and strummed "guitar", snaps, soft kick, clap, shaker, and a saw pad sidechained to the kick. The printer buzz, rip, whooshes, landings and key clicks are all placed from the composition's cue sheet. Normalized to -14.0 LUFS.
