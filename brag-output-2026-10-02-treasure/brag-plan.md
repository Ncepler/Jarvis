## Angle
A drawn treasure map through a clumsy website (Home, About us, Our team, Contact) to an X where 'how to book is in here'. The explorer digs up a chest with a calendar and a phone in it. Then the real barber demo hero, with its Book a chair button ringed on the first screen: 'Put the treasure on the first screen.'
Honesty: the map and the clumsy site are drawn jokes; the barber hero (Standard Barber Co., a demo brand) really has the Book a chair button on its first screen.
Sound: footsteps along the trail, pen squeaks at each stop, dig thuds, a chest creak, a loot chime, a ring bell, an end chord. -15 LUFS.
